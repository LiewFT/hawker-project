About
Makan Trail.

Makan Trail is a map of every hawker centre in Singapore, built around one rule: every record shows the date it was last checked.

How records are checked

A venue or stall listing carries the date someone last confirmed it. Once a record passes its re-check deadline it is labelled as stale instead of being shown as current. Stall listings are added only after being checked in person, and a missing stall does not mean it is not there.

What you will not find here

  • No star ratings, reader reviews or comments.
  • No invented content, and nothing copied from Google Maps or other sites.
  • No paid placements. Listings cannot be bought.

Where the data comes from

Hawker centre names, addresses and locations come from NEA's hawker centre dataset on data.gov.sg (Open Data Licence). The map is © OneMap, Singapore Land Authority. For opening hours and directions we link out to Google Maps rather than copy it.

Corrections

A public way to send corrections is not set up yet.
