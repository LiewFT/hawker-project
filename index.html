How we
review.

Every stall on Makan Trail goes through the same three steps before it's published.

01

We queue and taste

Same as any other customer — full price, no heads-up to the stall, no free samples.

02

We film and photograph

Every dish is shot on site. Video for the ones worth watching someone cook.

03

We publish it straight

The good, the average, and the "don't bother" — all get a review, not just the wins.
