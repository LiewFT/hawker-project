What we collect

Browsing the site needs no account. This is what we collect if you create an account to leave a review.

  • Your email address and a nickname you choose. Your password is never stored by us; the sign-in service keeps only a hash of it.
  • The reviews you post (a star rating and optional text), shown publicly with your nickname and the date. Your email address is never shown.
  • We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

  • To display your review and attribute it to your account
  • To moderate submitted content and prevent spam or fake reviews
  • To improve the site based on how it's used

What we don't do

  • We don't sell personal data to third parties.
  • We don't use your data for purposes beyond what's described here without asking first.

How your data is protected

Data travels over HTTPS and is stored encrypted at rest by Google. Passwords are hashed by the sign-in service and never visible to us. Your email address is kept only in the sign-in service, not in the public reviews database. This is provider-side encryption, not end-to-end encryption: the service provider can technically access what it stores.
